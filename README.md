Dr. Mohammadreza Forghani is a faculty member at the Department of Computer Engineering, Islamic Azad University, Najafabad Branch. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Engineering (Islamic Azad University, Isfahan, 2024), an M.Sc. in Computer Software Engineering (Ferdowsi University of Mashhad, 2002), and a B.Sc. in Computer Engineering (1998).

His research interests lie at the intersection of Intelligent Systems, Network Architecture, and Optimization. Dr. Forghani specializes in Software-Defined Networking (SDN), Network Traffic Engineering, and IoT integration. His current research focuses on leveraging Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Deep Learning (DL) to develop robust Meta-Heuristic algorithms for complex computational optimization problems in next-generation network architectures.
